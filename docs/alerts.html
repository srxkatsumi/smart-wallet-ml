🚨 Alerts

Only fires on a volatility anomaly: a VIX regime shift or a portfolio ETF moving far outside its normal range. Not a daily report.
Market leg: VIX regime
Per-asset leg: portfolio ETFs vs. their own ATR
Only the 7 portfolio ETFs are checked here (not the broader Watchlist). "Normal move" is each ETF's own 14-day ATR as a % of price, its typical daily range. "vs. ATR" is today's return divided by that normal range; ≥2.5× triggers an alert for that asset.
How this alert works

The two daily emails (Carteira Inteligente, Carteira BOT Experimentos) go out every day regardless of what happened in the market. This channel is different: it only fires when something is outside the normal range, so it's worth checking without having to read a daily email for signal.

Market leg VIX transitions into the high regime (>25) from a lower one, or jumps ≥15% in a single day even without crossing that line.
Per-asset leg Any of the 7 portfolio ETFs moves ≥2.5× its own 14-day ATR in a single day, rare enough (a few times a year per asset) to be worth flagging, in either direction.
Transition, not persistence The VIX check compares only the last two days, so if volatility stays high for a week, this fires once (on the first day), not every day the state persists.
Not model drift This is separate from the ADWIN/Page-Hinkley drift detectors used internally in research: those catch a model going stale, not a market event.

Either leg alone is enough to trigger, no need for both a market-wide and an asset-specific signal at the same time.

Before you go on This site is a personal study project applying Machine Learning to financial markets. The forecasts have no proven statistical significance and should not be taken seriously as a basis for decisions. Nothing here is financial advice.
