Dividend/share is the trailing annual amount paid per share; frequency (Monthly/Quarterly/Semi-annual/Annual) is inferred by counting payments in the trailing 12 months, so a recently-started or recently-cut dividend may be misclassified. "Accumulating" vs. "Distributing" applies to ETFs only and is inferred from whether the fund currently pays a dividend (accumulating share classes reinvest instead); individual stocks aren't classified this way. "No dividends" includes accumulating ETFs as well as assets that simply don't pay one.
