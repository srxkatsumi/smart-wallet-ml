Reading the charts

Price + SMA20 + SMA50 + Bollinger Bands: the daily closing price (EUR), with two moving averages (20-day and 50-day; when price sits above both, the short-term trend is up) and a shaded Bollinger Band (2 standard deviations around the 20-day average; price pushing outside the band suggests an unusually large recent move, not necessarily "cheap" or "expensive").

RSI (14-day): Relative Strength Index, 0-100. Above 70 is traditionally read as "overbought", below 30 as "oversold" (dashed reference lines), a momentum gauge, not a prediction.

MACD: difference between the 12-day and 26-day exponential moving averages (line), its own 9-day average (signal, dashed), and the gap between the two as a histogram. MACD above its signal line is a classic short-term bullish cue; below is bearish, again a momentum signal, not a forecast.

D+1 accuracy (cumulative): running accuracy of the model's D+1 directional calls for this asset over the same window; the dashed line at 50% is what a coin flip would get.
